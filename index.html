SpeakerCover EG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

SpeakerCover EG is an independent seller of speaker covers in Egypt. We are not affiliated with JBL. This policy explains how we handle information when you message us on Instagram or Facebook, or order from our store.

What we collect

  • Messages you send to our Instagram or Facebook accounts, and your public profile name.
  • Details you choose to share to place an order: name, phone number, delivery address, the product you want.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and process, deliver and support your order (cash on delivery).
  • Our internal tool reads incoming messages so we can answer faster. A person on our team reviews replies before they are sent.
  • We share delivery details only with our courier to deliver your order.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not use your messages for advertising profiles.

Retention

We keep message and order records only as long as needed to serve you, handle returns, and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and data deletion

To see, correct or delete your information, send the word DELETE in a message to our Instagram account @speakercover.eg or our Facebook Page. We will confirm and delete your records within 30 days, except where we must keep order records by law.

Contact

Message us on Instagram at @speakercover.eg or on our Facebook Page, SpeakerCover EG.
